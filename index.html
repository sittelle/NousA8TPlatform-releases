Adopting a NOUS A8T

From a factory plug running Tasmota, to Rescue, to a working Charge Guard install. Every step happens in a browser — Tasmota's own page, then the device's own pages after that.

AP address192.168.4.1
AP passwordNousA8TAdopted
Never runidf.py flash

What you need

Two files, downloaded from the latest release. Nothing else to install on this computer.
  • NousA8TAdoption.binUsed in step 2.
  • NousA8TChargeGuard.binUsed in step 5.
  1. Plug in the socket while it's still running Tasmota. Connect it to your Wi-Fi client (optional, but recommended).
  2. On Tasmota's own Firmware Upgrade page (http://<plug-ip>/up), upload NousA8TAdoption.bin.
  3. Stay on the page until it shows a decision to make, then choose:
    • Overwrite — continue to step 4.
    • Recovery — decide to recover to Tasmota instead; upload your own tasmota32.bin (get this from NOUS yourself); wait for Tasmota to come back up.
  4. On overwrite: wait for Rescue to come up.
  5. On Rescue's own page, install the Charge Guard app by uploading NousA8TChargeGuard.bin.
  6. Done.

Never run idf.py flash or a raw esptool write against an adopted device — only Rescue's own web interface keeps writes inside the partitions they're supposed to touch.
